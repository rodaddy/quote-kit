# Worked examples — see it work before you risk your own job

Four complete runs. Each one shows the real-shaped thing that went IN and the
finished thing that came OUT, end to end. Nothing here is a screenshot or a
summary — this is the actual output the kit produces.

Read one, then drop your own job in beside it and watch the same flow.

| File | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| `01-paver-patio-complete.md` | The full path: a customer email comes in, the kit asks for what's missing, prices it, checks the margin, writes the exclusions, numbers it, logs it. This is the everyday one. |
| `02-refused-underspecified.md` | **The kit saying no.** A one-line request that looks quotable and isn't. No number was assigned and no ledger row was written. This is the most important file in the folder. |
| `03-hourly-flat-mixed.md` | A quote that mixes a flat fee with hourly work, on a service business rather than a trade — proof the kit is not only for per-foot pricing. |
| `04-options-and-change-order.md` | **The two easiest ways to send out a wrong number.** "Give me a price both ways" priced as alternatives that never add together, on one quote number — then, three weeks into the job, a change order numbered `0068-A` that inherits the original's terms and tax and does not spend a new number. |

## A note on what these are NOT

These examples are here so you can see the flow before you commit. **They are
not your install test.** When you install the kit, it puts a job YOU actually
need to quote through it, with your customer's real name on it — because a
quote for a made-up customer proves nothing about whether it works for your
business. If the installer ever offers to test itself on the data in this
folder, it is doing the wrong thing.

## The names in these examples are invented

The businesses and customers here are made up for illustration. The prices,
the exclusions, and the New York sales-tax handling are realistic for Long
Island trade work, but they are examples, not advice — your rate card and your
accountant decide your numbers.
