# Start Here

You downloaded the Quote Kit as a zip. Here is how to get the one file that
matters — `installer.md` — in front of your AI. Pick your device.

## iPhone or iPad

1. Open the **Files** app and find the zip you downloaded.
2. Tap the zip once — it unzips into a folder with the same name.
3. Open the folder and tap **installer.md** to open it.
4. Tap the Share icon and send it into your **Claude** or **ChatGPT** app —
   or open the file, select all the text, copy it, and paste it into a new chat.

## Windows

1. Find the downloaded zip, **right-click** it, and choose **Extract All**.
2. Open the folder that appears.
3. Drag **installer.md** into your AI app or website (the Claude app,
   ChatGPT, Gemini) — or open the file, copy all the text, and paste it in.

## Mac

1. **Double-click** the zip — it unzips into a folder next to it.
2. Open the folder.
3. Drag **installer.md** into your AI app or website — or open the file, copy
   all the text, and paste it into a new chat.

---

Send that file to your AI and say: **install quote kit.**
